This is spoken Levantine Arabic (Lebanon, Syria, Palestine, Jordan), not the formal written kind. The spelling under each picture is just to help you remember: ' is a little catch in the throat, kh is like a French r, and gh is a softer gargle. Always copy the sound, not the letters. Phrases are said as a man would say them, to a man.

Each topic has three ways to practise: look (go through the phrases), listen (hear one, tap its picture) and say it (see the meaning, say it out loud, then check). The app keeps track of what you know: it never asks the same thing twice in a row, and mistakes come back a few questions later.
